## What is TalentBattles? {#introduction-what-is}

**Talent Battles** was born as a personal project with the intention of creating an application for artists
and apprentices from the world of music production and digital design.

The main purpose of this website is to offer a place that can help artists to progress
while they compete and have fun doing what they like best.

**Talent Battles** aims to be a place that is easily accessible to everyone, including visitors and talent seekers.
It is an ideal place to browse and search for creations, get inspiration or to find new contacts.
and business opportunities.

This application tries to differentiate itself from the rest by offering unique and original functionalities,
as are the battles and tournaments of creations.
It also tries to facilitate searches for the creations of its artists,
in addition to offering interesting statistics about their activity and performance.
