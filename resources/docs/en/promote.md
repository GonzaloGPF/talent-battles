# Promotion

---

Promotions are an excellent way to give more visibility to your Creations. If you think your Creation needs more attention and you want to make it more attractive, you can Promote it for a limited period of time

A Creation can be Promoted for months. The more months you promote your creation, the cheaper the investment will be.

You should know that it will not be possible to Promote a Creation that is already being Promoted.

- [Promote](#promote-promote)
- [Advantages](#promote-advantages)
- [Prices](#promote-prices)

## Promote {#promote-promote}
To Promote your Creations you must access your [Creations personal area](/admin/creations).
Once there, you must click on the **Promote** button and follow the steps.

You can Promote several Creations at the same time and choose the number of Months you want to keep the Creations Promoted

> [!NOTE]
> Promoting for more months grants a greater discount

## Advantages {#promote-advantages}
Here we list the advantages that your Creation will obtain
- Promoted: It will become a **Promoted** Creation, that is, it will appear on the main page of **Talent Battles**
- Stand Out: Promoted Creations will have a bright frame that will make them more eye-catching

## Prices {#promote-prices}
- The price to Promote a Creation is 5€ for each Month
- From 2 Months you will get a discount for each extra month (-5€ for each Month extra)
- The limit discount will occur after 6 Months
- The maximum discount that can be achieved is 20%
