# Contributors

---
All those who have contributed to building **Talent Battles** will be stored and remembered forever in the memory of our community.

- [Contribute](#contributors-contribute)

## Contribute {#contributors-contribute}

Currently, you can contribute to us through a Donation.
At the time of making a Donation you can also leave a personalized message and the system will show it in the
[List](/contributors) of Contributors.

If you are logged in at the time of making the Donation, your profile will also be linked to the Contribution, otherwise,
the Contribution will be **Anonymous**.

The list of Contributors will be random, that is, each time the page is visited, it will show a different list of Contributors.

> [!NOTE]
> Contributor's Users will be highlighted
