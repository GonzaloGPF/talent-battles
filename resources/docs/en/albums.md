# albums

---
You can group your **Creations** by [Albums](/admin/albums).
From this section you will be able to manage your Albums as well as be able to view a summary of each one.

- [Create Album](#albums-create)
- [Edit Album](#albums-edit)
- [View Album](#albums-view)

## Create Album {#albums-create}
To create an Album, all you have to do is press the 'Create' button and fill in the required fields.
With this, you will create a new Album that you can select later in the forms of your **Creations**.

## Edit Album {#albums-edit}
Each element of the list offers the possibility of being edited. You can modify any attribute of your Albums in
all time.

## View Album {#albums-view}
An Album is a way of grouping Creations that you have uploaded to **Talent Battles**. By clicking on the button
'view' you will be able to access a public page where all the elements of your Album will be shown to the whole world.
