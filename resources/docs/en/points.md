# Points

---

Points are the currency with which the **Talent Battles** economy works.
These Points are necessary to perform many of the actions of this application.
You can see how many Points you have at all times in the top menu.

Earning Points is very simple. It will contribute to gaining experience to level up and unlock achievements.

- [Earn Points](#points-earn)
- [Spend Points](#points-spend)
- [Buy Points](#points-buy)

> [!NOTE]
> Earning Points will always grant [Experience](experience.md)

## Earn points {#points-earn}

- Voting in Battles (**1 Points**)
- Voting for the Winner (**2 Points**)
- Winning a Battle (**30 Points**)
- Uploading a Creation (**2 Points**)

## Spend Points {#points-spend}
- Create a Battle (**20 Points**)

## Buy Points {#points-buy}
Although it is possible to earn Points for free, we have also wanted to offer the option of acquiring Points
through financial outlay. This option may allow you to save some time, but you will lose interaction with the
rest of users.

The minimum number of points to purchase is **50 Points** and the price of each Point is **0.1€**

> [!WARNING]
> Points purchased through purchase will not give [Experience](experience.md)
