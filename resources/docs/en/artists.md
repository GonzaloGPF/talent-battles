# Artists

---

**Talent Battles** is a place made up of creators of audiovisual material.

Each User will be able to share and test her talent in this community with the intention of having fun and improving her skills.

- [Designers](#artists-designers)
- [Producers](#account-producers)
- [Musicians](#account-musicians)
- [Singers](#account-singers)

## Designers {#artists-designers}
Designers are all those Users who have created [Designs](/designs)

## Producers {#account-producers}
Their Creations are the [Beats](/beats) and they specialize in producing
complete, both for singers and for any other purpose (soundtracks, advertising, background music, etc.)

## Musicians {#account-musicians}
It is also possible to provide [Samples](/samples), which are pieces of audio containing
a melody or a rhythm of a specific instrument. It is very useful for Producers to build beats

## Singers {#account-singers}
The Singers mix everything together to create [Songs](/songs). A final and complete product
