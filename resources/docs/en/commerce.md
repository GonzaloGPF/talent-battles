## Trade

---

In this section you will find everything related to your Products, Orders and Sales, that is, everything related to
the Market Place side of **Talent Battles**

- [Products](#products)
- [Orders](#orders)
- [Sales](#sales)

## Products {#products}
This tab shows a list with all the Products that you are selling, that is, all the elements that you have placed
on sale. Whenever you put an item up for sale, a Product will be created in this section.

From here, you can carry out a small administration on the products, such as modifying the price or exclusivity.

> [!WARNING]
> If you have sold an **exclusive** Product you will not be able to modify it.

## Orders {#orders}
Any purchase you have made will appear in this section. From here, you can access all the Products that
you have purchased, and you will be able to download its files whenever you want. Even if the author removes its Product.

It is also a place where you can see the status of your Orders,
useful to know if there were any errors.

## Sales {#sales}
Similar to the previous section, from here you will see a small summary with all the sales you make.
Here you can see the details of each sale, such as its sale price or the commission applied.

> [!NOTE]
> Remember that in order to sell anything you must activate your [Account](account.md).
