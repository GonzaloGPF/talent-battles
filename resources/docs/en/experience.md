# Experience

---
In **Talent Battles** all Users gain experience as they interact with the platform and perform different actions.

As a User gains experience, they will level up and earn different rewards.
It will also unlock achievements and other features.

- [Earn Experience](#experience-earn)
- [Levels](#experience-points)

## Gain experience {#experience-earn}
You will get as many experience points as [Points](points.md) you get, so the more Points you earn
the more Experience you will get and the faster you will level up.

## Levels {#experience-points}
Depending on your number of points, you will get the following Progress Levels:

- Novice: **0 Points**.
- Apprentice: From **500 Points**.
- Medium: As of **1000 Points**.
- Advanced: From **5000 Points**.
- Expert: From **10000 Points**.
