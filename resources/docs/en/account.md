# Account

---

**Talent Battles** systems integrate with [Stripe](https://stripe.com/) using your **Account**.

> [!NOTE]
> Stripe is an excellent platform for managing online payments/collections

You can _Activate your Account_ by accessing your [User Profile](/profile).

- [Activate Account](#active-account)
- [Configure Account](#configure-account)

## Activate account {#active-account}
To Activate your account you must access the [Account](/profile/account) section of your User Profile.

If you do not have the Account activated, you will see a button to **Activate** your Account.

After activation, **Talent Battles** will register you in [Stripe](https://stripe.com/) with all the information
that you have in your profile, that is, address, name, email, telephone, etc. Make sure that these data are correct to avoid problems or errors.

## Set Up Account {#configure-account}
Once you have created your Account, you can configure it by clicking on **Configure**. 
This will take you to the [Stripe](https://stripe.com/) gateway where will request relevant information about your tax profile, 
in order to validate your User as someone suitable to make Sales and receive money.

Once you complete the process, you will be redirected back to your [Account](/profile/account) 
and you will be able to check if everything went well.

Make sure the following information is completed:
- Email: Your email in your Stripe Account
- Details Sent: You have successfully sent your data to Stripe.
- Enabled Charges: Your Stripe Account allows you to transfer money.
- Enabled Payments: Your Stripe Account allows you to receive money.

> [!NOTE]
> Remember that, when making sales, you become a seller, you will be responsible for setting prices and applying taxes for your services.
