# Battles

---

Battles are the core functionality of **Talent Battles**. They are a mechanism to gain points and experience.

In addition to being a lot of fun, Battles will also help you measure yourself against other creators and improve your skills.

## Functioning {#battle-functionality}

A Battle lasts for **5 days**,
Once the term is over, the winner will be determined based on the number of points.

Each Creation will earn points with the Votes of the Users.
The winning Creation will be the one with the most points. The winning User of the Battle will be rewarded
with points and experience

## Create Battle {#battle-creation}
You can create Battles at any time, as long as you have the necessary Points
(**20** Points), via the Battle button.

There are three ways to participate in a Battle:
- Create a Battle by yourself
- A User challenges you
- **Talent Battles** pairs your Creation with another one of the same type, from another User. This way there are always Battles to vote on, and it costs you no Points

> [!NOTE]
> Only Creations that have the attribute **'can be challenged'** active can be challenged or paired

## Battle Types {#battle-types}
Currently, in **Talent Battles** there are two types of Battles:
- Tracks: Those composed of Beats, Samples or Songs. It will be necessary to listen to each track for a minimum time before being able to make a [Vote](battles.md#battle-votation)
- Designs: Those composed of Designs

## Restrictions {#battle-constraints}
There are a number of conditions when creating Battles:
- A Creation can only be in one active Battle at a time
- Create a Battle require **20** Points
  -A User can never vote in a Battle in which he participates
- Each User can only vote once per Battle

## Voting {#battle-votation}
A Battle will pit two Creations against each other in a showdown of talent and artistic ability
for a certain period of time (**5 days**)

Each User will vote for the Creation they consider best, granting **Voting Points**. The amount of **Vote Points**
that a User will contribute to a Creation will be determined by their [Experience](experience.md).

If you are not signed in, you can vote with your Google account: your vote counts as soon as you come back from Google.

> [!NOTE]
> You will not be able to Vote for yours Creations. You will not be able to vote until you verify your email.

### Draws {#battle-draw}
It is possible that, after the term of a Battle, the participating Creations end up with the same amount
of points. In such a case, the battle will end with no winners and it will be considered a **draw**.

Each participating User will receive **15 Points** for the effort of the Battle.

### Extra Points
Each vote you did, will give you **1 Points**

> [!NOTE]
> If you vot for the winner, you will earn triple points!
