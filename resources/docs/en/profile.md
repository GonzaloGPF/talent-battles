You will also be able to see a small summary of your public profile. {#profile}

### Personal information {#personal-data}
You can modify any of your personal data at any time. The only mandatory information is the email and the name.
If you do not wish to reveal any other data, you can always leave it empty.

> [!NOTE]
> If you don't want to show your **name**, you can specify a **nick**, by this way, it will be shown instead.

### Delete User {#delete-user}
From this same section you can delete your account completely by clicking the delete button. The system will ask you
confirmation before performing the action.

> [!CAUTION]
> Deleting your User will destroy all your data

### Social networks {#social-media}
You can fill in your social networks by pasting here the links to your different profiles.
When you fill in a social network, a small button will appear on your public profile for each social network, which will allow other Users to
locate you through these networks easily.

### Change Password {#change-password}
You can modify your password at any time, supplying your current password and typing a new password with confirmation.

## Notifications {#account-notifications}
You are on a social network with a lot of activity, so the best way to find out everything is through Notifications.
Notifications have the function of notifying you of any important event.
Make sure to properly configure your notifications [here](/profile).
There are several ways to receive notifications:
- Issue: It is a small Notification in the application itself
- Mail: This type of notification will come to your email

## Payment Methods {#payment-methods}
In this section you can manage all your payment methods. When you make card payments, the system will automatically save
your payment details to increase future payments speed.
From here you can add new methods, or if you wish, you can modify or delete any of the current ones.

### Default Payment Method
You can set a Default Payment Method by clicking on the star icon. This will convert the Payment Method to
Your favorite. When you have a Favorite Payment Method, a new button will be enabled at the time of [Process an Order](cart) that will allow you to
make the payment directly after a small confirmation.

## Account {#account}
This section will allow you to control the financial aspect of your profile. More information [here](account.md).
