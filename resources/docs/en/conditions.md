- Each User votes once per Battle and cannot vote in their own. The weight of each vote depends on the voter's experience level.
- Points are an internal unit of **Talent Battles**, used to create Battles and for other actions on the site. They are not money: they cannot be exchanged for money or moved to another account, and they are not refunded, except in the cases in [section 9](#terms-purchases).
