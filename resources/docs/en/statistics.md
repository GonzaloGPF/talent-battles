## Stats

---

This is the home page of your [Stats](/stats). From here you can see a summary of the most important.

- [Summary](#summary)
- [Balance](#balance)

## Summary {#summary}
In this section you will see useful information about the activity that the community has had with your profile. Next, 
We describe what the different zones consist of:

### Cards
- Tracks: Shows the total count of tracks you have uploaded. Broken down by type.
- Designs: Shows the total count of designs you have uploaded. It will display a breakdown of the four most numerous layout types.
- Battles: Summary of all the battles you've had
- Communicate: A small summary of some actions that other users have carried out on your creations.

### Pie Charts
- Tracks: Distribution by type of all Tracks you have uploaded.
- Designs: Distribution by type of all the Designs you have uploaded.
- Battles: Total Battles lost and won. Ongoing Battles will not appear here.

### Line Graph
This graph shows a summary of user activity against your creations, over the course of the last year.

## Balance {#balance}
This section is dedicated to your sales. It will only be available if you have your [Account](account.md) activated.

### Cards
- Creations: A summary of the creations you have sold. Split between Tracks and Designs.

### Bar Graph
Shows the total amount of sales and money earned, by month, from all your creations in the last year.
