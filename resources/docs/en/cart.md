# Cart

---

All the purchase operations you make from **Talent Battles** will be managed from **The Cart**.
**The Cart** will always be visible at the top left of the screen as long as you have at least one item added.

- [Add to Cart](#add-to-cart)
- [Remove Items](#remove-items)
- [Process Order](#process-order)
- [Complete Order](#complete-order)

## Add to Cart {#add-to-cart}
You can add items to the **Cart** by clicking the cart button on the different items you see in **Talent Battles**.
Items you add will be added to your Cart immediately and saved to your account until you decide to [Process Order](#process-order)
or delete them.

## Remove Items {#remove-items}
After adding items to your **Cart** you will be able to view a list from the upper right menu. From this menu you can delete the elements
by clicking the delete button for each item. You can also remove all items from your **Cart** at once by clicking the delete button.

## Checkout {#process-order}
This option will allow you to access the details of your ** Cart ** and is where you can ** Process the Order **.
You will be able to see a table with all the information of each element of the **Cart**, such as its description, price, discounts, taxes, etc.
To place the Order you must complete the payment process by clicking any of the following buttons:

### Pay with
This button will create a dropdown that will offer you different possible payment options. You must fill in all the fields
with the correct information to be able to Process the Order.

> [!NOTE]
> If you choose to fill in the information of a Credit Card and you are logged in, the system will save the payment data in your [Payment Methods](profile#payment-methods)

### Pay now
If you have any [Payment Method](profile#payment-methods) previously saved, this option will allow you to make the payment immediately, without the need
to refill any forms.

## Complete Order {#complete-order}
If after filling out the form correctly and sending it no error occurs, the system will create an [Order](orders) with all the items in the **Cart**.
You will be redirected to a small section with information on the Order and its Order lines, as well as downloadable files, if any.

The System will also send email to the email address that you have filled in when making the payment.

> [!NOTE]
> If you are logged in when placing the Order you will be able to access all your Orders from the [Order Management](/commerce/orders) section
