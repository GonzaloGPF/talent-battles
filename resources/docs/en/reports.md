# Reports

---
Please help us keep **Talent Battles** clean and safe. If you detect any type of inappropriate behavior, please report it by creating a Report

This will help our team to better moderate and manage this application.

All Complaints you make will be saved in your Complaints area and will be dealt with as soon as possible.

The more information you provide, the easier it will be for us to solve the problem. A screenshot can help better document your request. A picture is worth a thousand words!
