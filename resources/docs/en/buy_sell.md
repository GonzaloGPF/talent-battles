# Buy/Sell

---

**Talent Battles** is also a perfect place to Buy and Sell Creations.

You will be able to put any Creation up for sale by setting a price for it and the system will offer other Users the possibility of buying it.

Once a User purchases a Creation, they will have access to said Creation at any time and may download it whenever they wish.

It will only be possible to stop selling a Creation if it has not been previously sold.

- [Conditions of Sale](#sell-conditions)
- [Sell Of Exclusives](#sell-exclusive-conditions)
- [Purchase Conditions](#buy-conditions)
- [Commissions](#commissions)

## Conditions of Sale {#sell-conditions}

- Being the Author of Creation
- Upload the version without watermark
- Have the [Account](account.md) configured

> [!NOTE]
> It is important that each Creation for sale has the version without a watermark

### Sale of Exclusives {#sell-exclusive-conditions}
An Exclusive sale means that it is only sold once.
Once a User makes the purchase of an exclusive, no one else will be able to buy said Creation.
- You can sell a Creation exclusively as long as you have not previously sold it
- If a User buys an exclusive Creation, the system will prevent other Users from buying it

> [!NOTE]
> The price of an exclusive Creation is usually higher than that of a non-exclusive Creation

## Purchase Conditions {#buy-conditions}
- Not being the Author of Creation
- Have sufficient funds

## Commissions {#commissions}

When you make a sale, the system will apply a small commission of **10%** on the sale price.
